Projects of this scale and proximity typically require a Partial Environmental Impact Statement submitted to the State Ministry of Environment alongside your planning application.

  • Prepare an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS)
  • Engage an EIA-accredited consultant (see Network)
  • Public/community notice period may apply

Why this matters

Nigeria's EIA Act (Cap E12 LFN 2004) requires screening for projects with significant environmental impact before approval can be granted. Screening early avoids delays later in the approval process.
