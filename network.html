How verification works

  1. You submit your registration details.
  2. You add the payout account escrow will remit your earnings to.
  3. You pay the one-time verification fee to our account.
  4. Once payment is confirmed, we automatically check your professional body number against the relevant council's register.
  5. Verified professionals appear on the public Network directory with a "Verified" badge.

Regulatory bodies we check against

  • Engineering / Structural — COREN (coren.gov.ng)
  • Surveying — SURCON (surcon.gov.ng)
  • Architecture — ARCON (arcon.gov.ng)
  • Town Planning / Planning Services — TOPREC (toprec.gov.ng)
  • Estate Management — ESVARBON (esvarbon.gov.ng)
  • Construction — CORBON (corbon.gov.ng)
  • Environmental — EHORECON (ehorecon.gov.ng)
  • Quantity Surveying — QSRBN (qsrbn.gov.ng)

Where a council has no public online verification API, our compliance team completes manual verification within 2 business days and updates your status automatically.
