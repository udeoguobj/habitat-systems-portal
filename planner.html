Planning Project Planner

A step-by-step compliant pathway from land acquisition to certificate of occupancy.

1

Land Acquisition & Title Perfection

Verify title, obtain Governor's Consent / Certificate of Occupancy.

2

Survey & Site Analysis

Commission a registered surveyor for a beacon survey and site report.

3

Design & Documentation

Engage an architect/engineer to prepare drawings sealed by the relevant council.

4

Statutory Submission

Submit to the state planning authority via Doc Tracker.

5

EIA Screening (if required)

Run your project through EIA Tools before submission.

6

Approval & Permit Issuance

Receive your building/development permit.

7

Construction Monitoring

Track site compliance via the Web GIS portal.

8

Completion & Certificate of Occupancy

Final inspection and C of O issuance.
