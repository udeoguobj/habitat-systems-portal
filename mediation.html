Mediation

Either party can request mediation, but we only step in once both sides consent to us viewing the relevant chat and project data.

1. Mediation Initiation Form

Start here if you and the other party disagree on a milestone.

How mediation works

  1. Either party submits the initiation form above, with consent.
  2. The other party is notified and asked to also consent.
  3. Only once both consent do our mediators get read-only access to the chat, milestone history and payment status for that project.
  4. All mediator access is logged for audit.
  5. Once resolved, mediators mark the case closed and access is automatically revoked (form below).
